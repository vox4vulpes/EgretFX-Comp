    SIDECHAIN

    This browser can't run Egret Comp (it needs AudioWorklet). Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

    THRESH sets the level above which the signal is turned down; RATIO how much; the STYLE button picks a detector/topology character; ATTACK and RELEASE how fast gain reduction grabs and lets go (AUTO blends in a program-dependent release); MAKEUP trims the output, or let AUTO GAIN work it out; MIX blends the compressed sound back with the dry signal (parallel compression at low settings). KNEE softens the onset around the threshold; RANGE caps how much reduction can happen; LOOKAHEAD lets the detector catch a transient before it's heard, at the cost of a little latency; LINK ties the two channels' detectors together. SIDECHAIN routes another sound (pick one in the player) to the detector instead of the input, filtered by its own HPF and bell. Drag the dot on the curve to set threshold and knee. The meter on the right shows input, output and gain reduction (GR, from the top). Click a knob's value to type one. Space plays the source.
